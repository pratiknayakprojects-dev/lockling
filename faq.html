Privacy

Does Lockling upload my photos or passwords anywhere?

No. Everything is encrypted with AES-256 and stored only on your iPhone. Lockling has no servers and no account, and the vault is left out of iCloud and device backups.

Who can open Lockling?

Anyone who can unlock your iPhone with Face ID, Touch ID or the passcode. Lockling locks itself when you leave the app.

Pricing

What is free?

Your first 10 photos and videos, plus backup and restore. Lockling Pro adds unlimited photos and videos, Files, Secrets and sharing from other apps for $2.99 a month, $14.99 a year or $24.99 once (US prices).

What happens to my items if I cancel Pro?

Everything you've hidden stays viewable and exportable. You just can't add more beyond the free allowance.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Lockling Pro screen and tap Restore, or use Restore Purchases in Settings.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Lockling Pro.

Backups and recovery

I deleted the app. Can I get my vault back?

Only from a backup you exported. The vault exists only on your iPhone, so there is no other copy. In Lockling, go to Settings and tap Export Encrypted Backup.

I forgot my backup password. Can it be recovered?

No. The backup is encrypted with that password and nobody else can open it, including us.

How do I move Lockling to a new iPhone?

Export an encrypted backup in Settings, save it to Files or iCloud Drive, then use Restore from Backup on the new iPhone. This works without Pro.

Devices

Does Lockling work on iPad?

Lockling is made for iPhone. It runs on iPad in an iPhone-sized window.
