Lockling Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

The short version

Lockling does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything you put in the app stays encrypted on your iPhone. We have no servers that receive your content, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your device

Photos, videos, files, notes, passwords, keys, cards and other records you add are encrypted with AES-256 and stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone. The encryption key is kept in your iPhone's Keychain behind Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode, and never leaves the device. Lockling does not include your vault in iCloud or device backups.

Photos access

Lockling reads only the photos and videos you choose. If you agree, it asks iOS to move the originals to Recently Deleted after they are safely in the vault. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Camera and scanning

When you scan a document or card, the camera image is read on your iPhone using Apple's on-device text recognition. The scan and the text found in it go straight into your encrypted vault. Nothing is uploaded.

Sharing into Lockling

When you share a file to Lockling from another app, it is encrypted on your iPhone before it is saved, and appears in the vault the next time you unlock it.

Backups

Lockling only creates a backup when you ask it to. The backup is encrypted with a password you choose and saved wherever you pick. We never receive it and cannot open it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Data we collect

None. Lockling contains no tracking, analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDKs.

Deleting your data

Delete items inside the app, or delete the app to erase the whole vault. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, deleted data cannot be recovered.
