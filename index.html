Common questions

I deleted the app. Can I get my vault back?
Only from a backup you exported. Your vault exists only on your iPhone, so we have no copy. In Lockling, go to Settings and tap Export Encrypted Backup.

I forgot my backup password.
It can't be recovered. The backup is encrypted with that password and nobody else can open it.

Who can open Lockling?
Anyone who can unlock your iPhone with Face ID, Touch ID or the passcode.

How do I restore a purchase?
Open the Lockling Pro screen and tap Restore.

How do I cancel a subscription?
Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lockling Pro.
