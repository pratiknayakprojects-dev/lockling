Files

Secrets

Passwords, API and encryption keys, bank cards, IDs, Wi-Fi, licences and secure notes.

Scan to fill

Scan an ID or card and Lockling fills in the fields. The scan is read on your iPhone.

How it keeps things private

  • Nothing leaves your iPhone. Lockling has no servers and no account, and never sends your content anywhere.
  • AES-256 encryption for every item and thumbnail. The key stays in your iPhone's Keychain.
  • Opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. It locks when you leave and is hidden in the app switcher.
  • Left out of iCloud and device backups. You make your own password-protected backup when you choose.
  • No tracking. No analytics, ads or third-party SDKs. The App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected".

Who it's for
