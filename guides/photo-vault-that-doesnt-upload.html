Choosing a photo vault app that doesn't upload: 6 checks

Many "private" vault apps sync to their own cloud or need an account. If you want your photos to stay on your iPhone, check these before you trust one.

1. The App Store privacy label

Scroll to App Privacy on the app's page. "Data Not Collected" is the strongest answer. "Data Linked to You" means the developer receives something tied to your identity.

2. No account or sign-up

An app that needs an email or account usually stores something on a server. A truly offline vault has nothing to log in to.

3. The airplane-mode test

Turn on airplane mode, open the vault and add a photo. It should work fully. If it complains about the network, it depends on a server.

4. Real encryption, not just a PIN screen

Look for a named standard such as AES-256 and a key protected by Face ID or the Keychain. A lock screen alone doesn't protect files on disk.

5. Backups you control

Offline means no cloud copy. Make sure the app lets you export a password-protected backup and restore it on a new iPhone. Otherwise a lost phone means lost photos.

6. What happens if you stop paying

Some apps lock your photos behind the subscription. Check that you can still view and export everything if you cancel.
