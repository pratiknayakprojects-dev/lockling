How to hide photos on iPhone without iCloud

iPhone has a built-in way to hide photos, but if iCloud Photos is on, hidden photos still sync to iCloud. Here are your options and what each one actually does.

Option 1: The Hidden album (built in)

  1. Open Photos and select the photos.
  2. Tap the More button (…) and choose Hide.
  3. They move to the Hidden album, which opens with Face ID or your passcode.

The catch: with iCloud Photos turned on, hidden photos are still uploaded and appear hidden on your other Apple devices. Anyone who can unlock your iPhone can also open the Hidden album.

Option 2: Turn off iCloud Photos

Settings → your name → iCloud → Photos, then turn off syncing. Photos then stay on this iPhone only.

The catch: it applies to your whole library, not just private photos, so you lose cloud backup for everything else too.

Option 3: An offline encrypted vault

A vault app moves chosen photos out of your library into its own encrypted storage, so the rest of your library can keep syncing as normal. Pick one that doesn't need an account and doesn't upload. Our checklist covers what to look for.

Lockling works this way:

  1. Tap Hide Photos and pick photos or videos. Only the ones you pick are shared with Lockling.
  2. They're encrypted with AES-256 and stored only on your iPhone, outside iCloud and device backups.
  3. Lockling offers to move the originals to Recently Deleted in Photos. Remove them from there too if you don't want them to linger for 30 days.
